Courses

These are a few of the courses I teach at the faculty.

COURSE 01

Fundamental of Site Surveying

The general aim of the course is to provide sufficient knowledge and understanding of basic principles in site surveying pertaining to building construction. The course covers the element of introduction to land surveying, linear surveying, compass surveying, levelling and contouring with emphasis on the process and techniques of the works.

View course information →
COURSE 02

Construction Information Technology

This course will cover how computers can be beneficial in the construction industry. The use of computers especially in the digital era can assist the works done in the construction industry. Computer applications that are regularly used in the construction industry and their basic functions will be demonstrated. Students will then have a hands-on experience to use the applications to complete the tasks in the form of real-life documents e.g., report, spreadsheet, schedule, computer-aided design (CAD) drawings.

View course information →
COURSE 03

Building Technology III

The general aim of the course is to provide sufficient knowledge and understanding of basic principles in building construction. The course covers the element of building finishes, external works, temporary works and building defects.

View course information →
COURSE 04

Construction Safety and Health

The course develops an awareness of the paramount importance of health, safety, and protection of environment in construction. It covers the understanding of development of construction health and safety policy, plans, and procedures. The course includes identifying the health hazards and understanding the risk assessment and control.

View course information →
COURSE 05

Site Surveying

The general aim of the course is to provide sufficient and up to date knowledge related to site surveying for construction industry. The course covers introduction to site surveying, introduction to levelling, total station and traversing, tachometry, setting out, areas and volume and GIS, with emphasis on the technique and process of the works. Students will be equipped with basic skill in conducting and organizing field survey team.

View course information →
COURSE 06

DBT683 — Forensic Engineering

This course deals with the introduction to forensic engineering for the assessment and solution of building engineering construction failures.
