SELECTED WORK

Research interests

01 / RESEARCH

Construction Education & Professional Knowledge

My research interests focus on construction education, professional knowledge, and the relationship between academic learning and industry practice. I am interested in how knowledge is developed, shared, and applied in the construction industry..

02 / DEVELOPMENT

Construction Methods & Materials Development

My development interests focus on improving construction methods, building materials, and practical systems. This includes exploring ways to improve material performance, construction processes, resource efficiency, and the application of technical knowledge to industry challenges.

03 / INNOVATION

Construction Innovation & Technology

My innovation interests are reflected in undergraduate projects I supervise, covering sustainable building materials, construction automation, occupational safety, noise reduction, and rainwater harvesting. These projects explore practical solutions to challenges in construction and the wider built environment. .
