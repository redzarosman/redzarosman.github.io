CONSULTATION EXPERIENCES

Areas of interest

01

Disaster Recovery Plan

The Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) Development Project with MADA Kedah involved consultancy work to develop a disaster recovery framework for water infrastructure management. The project focused on identifying potential risks, strengthening stakeholder coordination, and establishing recovery strategies to support infrastructure resilience, minimise service disruptions, and ensure the continuity of critical water resource management operations.

02

In-House Training on Property Development Process

The Property Development Process Training for Sime Darby Property Berhad was a two-day in-house training programme conducted by UiTM to enhance participants’ understanding of the property development lifecycle. The training covered key stages of property development, including planning, project implementation, regulatory requirements, and the practical application of relevant industry procedures and documentation.
